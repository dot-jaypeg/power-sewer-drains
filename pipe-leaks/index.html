FAQ

Common questions.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Shut off your main water valve right away to stop the flow. If water is near outlets or electrical, keep clear and turn off power to that area if it's safe. Then call a licensed plumber to repair the pipe.

Where is my main water shutoff valve?

In most Southern California homes it's on the outside of the house near where the water line comes in, often close to a hose bib on the front or side wall. There is also a shutoff at the water meter, usually in a box near the curb.

What causes pipes to leak or burst?

Common causes are corrosion in older pipe, high water pressure, worn fittings and joints, and pipes that shift or get damaged. Pinhole leaks in copper line are often caused by corrosion over time.

Can a leaking pipe be repaired, or does it need to be replaced?

Many leaks can be fixed by repairing or replacing the damaged section. If the pipe is badly corroded in several places, replacing a longer run may make more sense. We'll show you what we find and quote both options upfront.

How much does pipe leak repair cost?

It depends on where the leak is and how much pipe is affected. Visual estimates are free, and you'll get the price before any work starts.
