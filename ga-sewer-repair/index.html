FAQ

Common questions.

What are the signs of a broken sewer line?

Common signs include several drains backing up at once, sewage coming up in a tub or shower, gurgling drains, sewer odors, and soggy or unusually green patches in the yard. A camera inspection confirms the cause.

What is trenchless sewer repair?

Trenchless repair fixes or replaces a sewer line through existing access points instead of digging a trench along the whole pipe. Methods include cured-in-place pipe liners, epoxy pipe lining, and pipe bursting or boring.

What's the difference between a pipe liner, epoxy lining and pipe bursting?

A pipe liner cures a new pipe inside the old one. Epoxy lining coats the inside of the existing pipe to seal cracks and joints. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the path of the old one, breaking the old pipe apart as it goes. The camera footage tells us which fits your line.

How much does a sewer camera inspection cost?

Sewer camera inspections start at $350. You see the footage, and we quote the repair upfront before any work starts.

How do you decide which sewer repair I need?

We run a camera through the line first, show you what we find, and recommend the repair that fits: a liner, epoxy lining, pipe bursting or a spot repair. You get the price before we start.
