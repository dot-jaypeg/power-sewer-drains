FAQ

Common questions.

How much does water heater repair or installation cost?

It depends on the type and size of the unit and what's wrong with it. We start with a free visual estimate and give you the price for the repair or the new installation before any work starts.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If the unit is fairly new and the problem is a part like a thermostat, element, valve or burner, a repair usually makes sense. If the tank itself is leaking, or the unit is older and needing repeated repairs, replacement is usually the better value. We'll quote both so you can decide.

How long does a water heater last?

Traditional tank water heaters typically last around 8 to 12 years, depending on water quality and maintenance. Leaks from the tank, rusty hot water and rumbling noises are common signs one is near the end.

What should I do if my water heater is leaking?

Turn off the cold water supply valve on top of the water heater, and turn off the gas or power to the unit. Then call a licensed plumber to find where the leak is coming from.

Do you install new water heaters?

Yes. We remove the old unit and install the new one, with the price quoted upfront before any work starts.
